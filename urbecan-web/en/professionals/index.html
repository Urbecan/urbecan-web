Strategy / Sales / Results
Experience since 2003

A development needs more than visibility.

It takes planning, market knowledge and the capacity to manage many sales at once. We put our experience in new-build property at the service of every project.

How we work
each development.

We analyse the product, location, prices and target audience to define a sales strategy tailored to the development.

We prepare the project presentation, the sales material and the channels to reach the right buyers.

A dedicated team handles enquiries, viewings and follow-up with every prospective buyer.

We coordinate reservations, contracts and paperwork with the developer, the lenders and the notary.

We track the development’s progress and share regular reports until every sale is completed.

Behind every stage there is a complete team. From Diverso ↗, our business centre, we have in-house support in multimedia, marketing, legal, notarial and financial services.
